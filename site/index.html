deadvox

A singleplayer voxel survival prototype in the spirit of DayZ and Cataclysm: DDA. Streamed half-metre blocks, block editing, a day and night cycle, compressed time, and data-driven content with an HTML inventory.

  • Play (dusk, 19:30)
  • Seed 42
  • Start at noon
  • Dead of night
  • View distance 64 m
  • View distance 128 m

Debug mode adds keys: T compresses time, N makes a noise that interrupts it, U toggles a pretend danger that makes it unsafe.
