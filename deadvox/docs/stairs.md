# Explicit storeys, stairs and root cellars

ASCII layers remain the floor plans. Optional named storeys and stair access generate ordinary blocks for a flight, avoiding hand-maintained treads and overhead holes across layers. The generated blocks, not a separate route object, are placed in the site. See `src/core/stairFlight.ts`, `planFlight`, and `constructFlight`.

Template coordinates are blocks; site placement remains in metres. Storey height is standing-feet height, while layer indices identify support layers. Placement lowers the template origin for below-ground floors. Air cells in the authored template carve the cellar consistently, including across chunk borders; the surrounding terrain and structure remain. See `src/core/templates.ts`, `compileTemplate`, and `src/core/authoredSite.ts`, `AuthoredSite.stamp`.

Validation checks standing clearance and reachable floor space through openings, doors and stairs. This is a construction check, not runtime pathfinding. Doors remain openable for validation, while other solid furniture blocks a route. An upper-floor opening must stay inside the incoming flight's footprint and headroom, or it leaves a gap at the top of the stairs. See `src/core/templateSpatial.ts`, `upperFloorOpeningIssues`.

Buried rooms need world-derived sky visibility because wide ambient occlusion retains a nonzero ambient floor. Cellars share the site-structure discovery path with above-ground rooms; a below-ground template flag is not a lighting selector. Fields remain local to their geometry rather than spanning empty gaps. Conservative readiness and limited residency can darken a distant room, so the witness must prove both local darkness and correct field selection. This is bounded diffuse transport, not photometry. A material comparison needs current published fields, otherwise successive screenshots can mistake discovery or revalidation for a material or ambient-occlusion defect. Arrival observations, geometry settlement and pixel comparisons keep their full viewport. Initial mesh readiness remains independent of lighting completion. See `src/core/skyTransport.ts`, `buildSkyResult`, and `test/browser/stairs.mjs`, `lightingCellarProofs`.

Shamblers do not navigate stairs as routes. A resident can drift onto a flight while beelining, but a target on another floor does not grant stair knowledge. Door bashing remains deferred under #273 until mob and obstacle strength exist. See `src/core/zombies.ts`, `ZombieSystem.tick`.

The first-mesh reference restores main's site, lighting and worker sources on the same fixture/content and browser, isolating startup admission from geometry changes. It measures delivered geometry, not first rendered pixels, and is not a pristine whole-build screenshot comparison. Both timestamps remain observations rather than default timing assertions. Fetch the exact event base: a shallow merge checkout's parents need not contain that commit. Normalize the directory URL before identifying source modules: a trailing separator must not turn the overlay into a silent branch-source run. The reference rejects absent engine/streamer overlays and records applied sources and browser errors. Enter Play before waiting for meshes: the title screen does not construct the played world. See `test/browser/sky-main-arrival.mjs`, `measureMainFirstMesh`.

The performance gate needs a real GPU on the immutable published preview. Software-rendered CI establishes correctness, ownership and admission fairness, not a frame-rate verdict. Its relative job trace remains useful for locating candidate selection, snapshot preparation, admission, execution and publication delay; dispatch and return gaps also contain delivery and scheduling, not pure transfer. See `test/browser/sky-timing.mjs`, `summarizeSkyTiming`.

The debug route flag drives the controlled camp through the input and noclip owners, not direct pose writes or a second simulation loop. Forwarded holds retain native modifier state and release only their own shared keys, so reversing direction cannot cancel a still-owned sprint hold. See `src/game/inputBindings.ts`, `forwardedActionHolds`. Its launch link owns the complete diagnostic preset; offering the flag independently in the public launcher would mix uncontrolled scenarios into the hardware gate. Launch through `src/debug/skyRoute.ts`, `skyRouteUrl`, click Play and keep the mouse still. A wrong start, interruption or motion-budget exit produces partial evidence rather than completion. The console and overlay share a copyable JSON snapshot with renderer identity, actual dimensions, frame/job denominators and resolved versus censored camera-fallback and revalidation episodes. Outstanding jobs remain censored even if replies arrive after the route boundary. Empty denominators mean unavailable evidence, not success. A route rejected before motion has no geometry evidence: probing an unrelated scene adds work without earning useful-work coverage. See `src/debug/skyRouteMetrics.ts`, `skyRouteSummary`.

Lighting measurements cover CPU service plus owned texture, uniform and program submission. GPU lighting execution is unavailable, never a zero-cost claim. Geometry probes run after measurement and use the uninstrumented sampler so they cannot improve its service numbers. The observer must preserve the frame handler's continuation result even when nothing draws; it does not own scheduling. See `src/debug/skyRouteObserver.ts`, `observeSkyRoute`, and `src/game/frameDriver.ts`, `observeRealFrame`. BR owns the hardware gate judgment; neither these observations nor default tests declare acceptance.

The stairs site is a demonstration fixture, not the final Beat 1 house or Dad's Beat 3 cabin. Browser traversal proves player physics with noclip off; lighting checks are separate pixel witnesses. A screenshot alone does not prove floor support. Ladders and roof hatches remain a separate design in [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md).
