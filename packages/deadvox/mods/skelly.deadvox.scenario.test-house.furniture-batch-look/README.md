# Furniture batch look

Open `/?start-mod=skelly.deadvox.scenario.test-house.furniture-batch-look#{debug:true}`.

The fixture puts ten aperture checks beside the spawn. Three shut hinged windows align along the view to expose pane blend order. Two shut detailed doors, a boarded window, a boarded door, and a shut hatch exercise their closed parts. The loose comparison door and window begin shut: open each with its normal interaction, then close it again.

Check that every closed part looks the same after an open-then-closed cycle, including the boards and detailed leaf. Fit the supplied door bar to the unboarded detailed door and check its bar survives the same cycle. Look through the aligned panes: their glass must blend correctly where they overlap.
