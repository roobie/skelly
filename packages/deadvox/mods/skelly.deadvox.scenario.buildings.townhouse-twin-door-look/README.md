# Townhouse twin door look

Open the [fixture](/?start-mod=skelly.deadvox.scenario.buildings.townhouse-twin-door-look#{debug:true}).

The body starts at noon just inside the street door of the brick two-storey townhouse over a cellar, on the near
street of the townhouse look, facing west along the front band, with every skill at its top level from the start
kit. Why the treads stand on a spandrel and the door moved east is under `packages/deadvox/docs/building-pathway.md`,
"Worked example: city townhouse".

## Check

1. The street door is on your right, east of the stair. Ahead on your left, the house flight's treads climb east against a
   stepped plaster spandrel, solid from the floor up to each tread, and nothing hangs in the air.
2. Walk out through the door and back in: the yard path before it is clear and the front band inside is wide
   enough to turn in.
3. Walk round the stair's east end into the room behind it, then west along the lane to the stair's foot and climb
   the treads to the upper storey.
4. Come back down and take the cellar flight from the street storey: you keep your headroom under the treads all the
   way down.
5. From the street, compare the front with the cellarless two-storey house next door: the twin's door stands east
   of its two front windows.
