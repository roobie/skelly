# Fall-drop look

Open the [fixture](/?start-mod=skelly.deadvox.scenario.test-house.fall-drop-look#{debug:true}).

At noon, a heavy crate stands at the outer edge of a raised plank floor. The player starts beside it with the fixture kit and every skill at its top level. Push the crate from the edge and watch it drop to its landing. The rule and reason are in `packages/deadvox/DESIGN.md`, "Moving furniture".
