# Optic reference envelopes

Optics are static, stable-ID assembly attachments. No glass, reticle, zoom, night-vision, or thermal behavior is simulated. `mini-reflex` keeps its persisted ID but now means a **closed-emitter micro dot**, not an open RMR. Dimensions are visual classes, not manufacturer CAD. `1u = 11.5mm`; catalog `envelopeU` is the **measured solid envelope**, including feet, rings and controls. The table uses **L × H × W**, unlike manufacturers' usual L × W × H.

## Shapes and display

Tubular optics, scope bells, turrets and rings use **revolved solids**, not octagonal extrusions. Each scope's hollow profile includes its ocular flare, main tube and objective flare in one solid. The thermal follows the same tubular construction. Rings are hollow revolved bands around the tube. Bases, the prism body and holographic hood remain box/extruded geometry. The TA31-class prism now has a short flared round objective, a smaller protruding round ocular, a squat chamfered housing with a low illumination ridge, elevation/windage caps, and a narrow low bridge base with a cross-bolt clamp. Its overall bounds are unchanged.

The viewer defaults to **16 facets** (also selectable with `?facets=16`); six visibly polygonal facets were insufficient at optic-view distances. Sixteen keeps the round silhouette low-poly: each full scope has 856 triangles including its bases and turrets, versus 1,240 at 24 facets. `?facets=24` remains available for close-ups. Collision still uses the fixed eight-facet hull and ignores hollows; ring/tube intersections within one part are intentional. Export's caller-selected facet count is unchanged.

The micro dot is one closed, rounded-corner housing silhouette on a compact foot. Its floor, side walls and sloped front hood enclose a **rear-to-front window tunnel**; the body is not a filled prism or a front-pane-only frame. Two small round adjustment dials are on the rear-left side.

## Receiver mounting

Active generated defaults **and published designs/fixtures** use receiver rails. All optic types offered by a regular rifle template share receiver slot 3, at local X = -8u (AR: -6u, on its -12…0u receiver rail). A type-only swap therefore needs no connection rewiring. The heavy receiver keeps its separate receiver stations; the pistol deliberately uses the slide rail. Handguard/scout mounts are not supported by this optic catalog.

`optic-mount-fit` checks more than rail metadata: samples from the **actual resolved sight's foot bottoms** must sit on physical receiver/slide top solids, and every foot must stay inside the body's own X extent, excluding rails and action handles. A missing foot or empty footprint cannot pass; the physical foot span must meet the catalog's contact-length requirement. Catalog dimensions are requirements, not substitute geometry. Tests independently check resolved top faces and body bounds for each compatible template/type, exercise the real curated JSONs without moving their connections, and inject floating and missing feet into `sight.build` with unchanged ports/axes.

On a top-loaded bolt receiver the physical rail has **two bases**, rear X = -14…-9u and front X = -4…-2u, leaving the -9…-4u loading opening clear at the receiver top. Long-optic feet sit at -14…-13u and -3…-2u. On these hosts the prism also uses two separate feet, reaching its round ends without a bridge across the opening; its body and optical axis do not move. `sight.mountFeed` inherits the host's feed through its base. Other hosts retain the approved prism bridge. Receiver mounting takes precedence over chasing longitudinal eye relief; the existing cheek-line check remains.

The roof-mouth footprint X=-9…-4u, Z=-1.5…1.5u, through the 0.5u roof up to the rail top (normally Y=2…2.5u), is closed to every family, including optic bodies. Above it (to Y=9u), only recognized optic body solids—tube, housing, turrets and controls—may bridge; feet, bases, rings, bridges and unclassified sight solids may not. The core checks the closed mouth and other families; a mandatory gun-domain refinement checks the **actual resolved sight solids** in the upper footprint. Its catalog ID allow-list identifies body roles, never substitutes nominal geometry. Floating/missing-foot and actual loading-obstruction canaries enforce both checks.

**Cartridge loading past the optic body is deferred to g33's tubular receiver and real port**, not claimed safe here. A conservative 45° right-side swept path, tangent to the real top-opening right lip, uses a .308 maximum head/rim diameter of 0.473in (12.0142mm; [SAAMI rifle drawings](https://saami.org/technical-information/cartridge-chamber-drawings/)) plus a 0.25u allowance, grid-ceiled to 1.5u diameter. Its center starts at Y=3.25u, Z=1.5u (bottom at the actual Y=2.5u lip), then travels 6u up/out at 45° over X=-9…-4u. Current LPVO/high-mag would require 2.25u lifts, prism 1.5u, thermal 1u, chiefly because the side controls intrude. These are measurements for g33, **not applied height changes** or a present cartridge-clearance guarantee.

Compact dots, the holographic and fixed prism permit the fixed iron-sight silhouette in their static sightline. This represents a usable shared field of view, not simulated co-witness/optical focus. LPVO/high-mag rings are low by default: axes drop from 48.875 mm to **23 / 34.5 mm above the rail**, with an actual objective-bell gap of **2.875 mm**. These minima are below the rough 38–45 mm medium-ring reference: no extra spacer is added solely to meet that reference. `sight.mountSection` inherits the receiver's section through its base. The AR rail's forward receiver station lets the LPVO ocular begin at X=-16u, ahead of charging-handle travel (-18…-16u), without moving any foot onto the handguard. Only the A2 sightline then forces an AR LPVO lift: axis **34.5 mm**, down from 48.875 mm (the fixed front post requires 11.5 mm above the default low position). The rules are unchanged and the feet stay receiver-mounted. The high-mag is not an AR-template default; its AR preview still uses a separate lift to clear the handle.
